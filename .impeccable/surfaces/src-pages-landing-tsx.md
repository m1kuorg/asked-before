# Landing page

Scope: the public landing page at `/`. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: students and junior developers who keep re-asking a chatbot the same how-to, and hackathon judges arriving cold. Action: "Try it as a guest", which opens the shared demo workspace with no account. Proof: the demo history's own phrasings, the privacy table, and real interface fragments labelled Demo data or Example. Constraints: CSP `default-src 'self'`, self-hosted fonts, no invented claims. The direction was dealt by design-taste-frontend (`.taste-skill/direction.json`, seed 894356784). The user skipped impeccable's world round on 2026-10-23.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your own nine phrasings of one question become the type itself; repetition is the picture. It refuses the AI-learning SaaS hero (gradient, chat bubbles, icon feature cards).

OWN-WORLD: Type and nothing else on the first screen; further down, pictures arrive large and few, as relief from the type, as the dealt sheet says. Those are four credited archival photographs and one muted 1946 exam newsreel loop with a pause control, all in one treatment on white chapters. On the dark chapters the same photographs return as ASCII fields, rebuilt from the demo's own phrasings in the manner of ascii-magic.com, cleared under every text. Every chapter is at least one viewport tall. Added 2026-10-24 at the user's request. Full-bleed chapters of one flat colour each: plum #470c35, plum-black #0d060b, plum surface #1b0e17, white with ink #101421. Hot pink #ff488b only on the primary action and the data marks. Schibsted Grotesk 800 at display size with -0.05em tracking and 0.88 leading, against plain text. Square corners, no rules, no shadows; app fragments keep the app's radii.

STORY: The visitor recognises their own habit in "Asked before?", sees nine wordings of one question, types their own into the live input, learns that a cold rep without AI is the only proof, sees exactly what leaves the computer, then tries the guest workspace.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The plum field fills the screen. "Asked before?" sits as a two-line white h1 at 14vw from the left margin, filling the top 55%. The wordmark is top left; Docs, Log in and a pink "Try it as a guest" button are top right. One 20px supporting sentence sits bottom right in a 24vw column.

FORM: design-taste-frontend direction "type-only-statement", hero "Colour field, two-ink statement" made single-ink, motion "Colour by chapter". The type was redrawn from General Sans to Schibsted Grotesk for licence reasons. seed: none. The roll was skipped, not unavailable: on 2026-10-23 the user said "Skip impeccable visual world, use /design-taste-frontend".
